# **Web Design**
> one of the ways of improving the appeal of a website or any product is good design.
> To understand good design, we have to understand Web design principles like;
> - Color Theory
> - Typography
> - User Interface Design
> - User Experience Design
> These are the pillars of good web design.
#### Color Theory
> It's the art and science of creating the right pallet for your website. The color pallet tells a story, and the pallet you use should convey a message to your target audience.
> - Red: Love, Energy, Intensity
> - Yellow: Joy, Intellect, Attention
> - Green: Freshness, Safety, Growth
> - Blue: Stability, Trust, Serenity
> - Purple: Royalty, Wealth, Femininity
> The important is you make a conscious decision when choosing a pallet.
> Designers rely on scientific ways to colors:
> - Analagous colors [good for navbars, body and log and its bg], its not good at standing out.
> - Complementary Colors [brings out each of the colors.], take note not to style text and their bg with complementary colors, it's called color discord. Good for logos or icons
> - Triadic colors
> - perfect square

#### Typography
>Fonts are important because they affect meaning so much. The main font families to know are,
>- The Serif typeface - They make designs seem more authoritative or serious, traditional, stable
>- Sans-serif typeface - They have perfect right angles, they seem more novel, friendly, simple. They are highly readable or legible (e.g humanist, grotesque) 
>Using lots of fonts can make for a design that is cluttered or over complicated. Keep it down to 2 fonts. Go for fonts with similar mood, time era, Contrast the serifness, change the weights to create contrast.
>Run way from: Kristen, Comic Sans, payrus, viner, curlz.

#### User Interface (UI) Design
> - Hierarchy - Using hierarchy the most important information is conveyed first.
> 	 Ways to convey hierarchy: color (this is where complementary colors shine), Size (Bigger items often draw attention), Layout(Use around 40 to 60 chars per line), Alignment(Position of each element on the screen relative to each other. also reduce the number of align points), White space (empty space around texts or elements, clutter makes products look cheap, more space makes the design a lot more minimalist or elevated), Audience (take into consideration your target audience, for instance don't use black to design a poster for a kids concert.)

#### User Experience (UX) Design
> This refers to how users are going to interact with your site in the most convenient way possible.
> -  Simplicity - Just keep it simple
> - Consistency - Keep both design and functionality and consistent.
> - Reading Patterns - this is important because it helps us understand where to place important stuff. You can use the F pattern, Z pattern.
> - All Platform Design - It's important to thing about responsiveness when designing modern websites. Try to avoid so many banners.
> - Don't Use Your Powers for Evil (😂) - Dark patterns (patterns which get users to perform an action that is not beneficial to the user.)
